## Warning on affinity laws

Affinity laws are useful scaling relationships, not a replacement for the actual pump map. Their ideal assumptions include similar operation, non-cavitating behavior and essentially incompressible fluid through the pump. The closed hydraulic system still needs a compressibility/leakage/actuator model.
