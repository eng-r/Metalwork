## Modeling priority

For the simulator, mechanistic force coefficients calibrated from lab data are preferable to handbook-only coefficients. Handbook/manufacturer data remain useful for physically reasonable operating envelopes, initial RPM/feed scheduling, and tool-protection constraints.
